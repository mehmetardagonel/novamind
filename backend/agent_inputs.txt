=============================================================================
MASTER LIST OF AI AGENT INPUTS
=============================================================================
This file contains natural language commands to trigger every capability 
of the Novamind Email Agent.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. INBOX & READING (The "Inbox Agent")
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------

[Basic Listing]
- "List my connected email accounts"
- "Show me my latest 5 emails"
- "Check my inbox"
- "Do I have any new mail?"

[Filtering by Sender]
- "Show me emails from 'john@example.com'"
- "List emails from Google"
- "Find messages from my boss"

[Filtering by Date/Time]
- "Show me emails from today"
- "What emails did I get yesterday?"
- "List emails from the last week"
- "Show me emails from the last 3 months"

[Filtering by Topic/Keyword]
- "Show emails about 'Project Alpha'"
- "Find emails with subject 'Invoice'"
- "Search for 'meeting' in my emails"

[Filtering by Importance]
- "Show me my important emails"
- "Do I have any urgent messages?"
- "Check for important emails from today"

[Summarization & Insights]
- "Summarize my latest emails"
- "Give me a summary of the email from 'alice@example.com'"
- "What is the main point of the last email I received?"
- "Summarize my inbox for today"

[Deep Search / RAG Queries] (Uses intelligent search to find specific info)
- "What did Bob say about the deadline?"
- "Did I receive the contract yet?"
- "Find the login code sent by GitHub"
- "When is my next meeting?"
- "What are the key takeaways from the marketing team updates?"

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. DRAFTING (The "Draft Agent")
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------

[One-Shot Creation] (Provide all details at once)
- "Draft an email to 'client@company.com' with subject 'Proposal' and body 'Attached is the file.'"
- "Write an email to 'mom@gmail.com' saying 'Happy Birthday!'"

[Interactive Creation] (Agent will ask for missing details)
- "I need to write an email to 'boss@company.com'" (Agent asks: What should it say?)
- "Draft a follow-up email about the interview" (Agent asks: To whom?)

[Auto-Generation] (Agent invents subject/body from context)
- "Draft a polite decline to 'recruiter@agency.com' regarding the job offer"
- "Generate a thank you email to 'interviewer@tech.com' for their time yesterday"

[Managing Drafts]
- "Show all my drafts"
- "Show drafts for 'client@company.com'"

[Updating Drafts]
- "Update the draft to 'client@company.com' and make it more formal"
- "Change the body of the draft to 'bob@example.com' to say we are meeting at 3 PM"
- "Add a closing line to the draft for 'alice@example.com'"

[Deleting Drafts]
- "Delete the draft to 'spam@example.com'"
- "Remove the draft I wrote to 'old.client@test.com'"

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. SENDING (The "Send Agent")
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------

[Immediate Send] (Sends immediately without saving as draft first)
- "Send an email to 'colleague@work.com' right now saying 'I am running late'"
- "Immediately send a message to 'team@project.com' with subject 'Update' and body 'Done.'"

[Sending Existing Drafts]
- "Send the draft to 'client@company.com'" (Agent will ask for confirmation)
- "Confirm and send the draft to 'boss@company.com'"

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. ORGANIZATION (The "Organization Agent")
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------

[Moving Emails]
- "Move all emails from 'newsletter@shop.com' to 'Promotions'"
- "Organize my inbox by moving emails from 'jira@atlassian.com' to the 'Work' folder"

[Cleaning Up]
- "Delete all spam emails"
- "Clean up my spam folder"

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. MULTI-STEP / COMPLEX WORKFLOWS
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
- "Find the email from 'Client X' about the budget, summarize it, and then draft a reply saying we accept."
- "Check if I have any emails from 'HR', and if so, move them to the 'Admin' folder."
